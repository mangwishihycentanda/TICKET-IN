## 1. What Ticket-In is — and isn't

Ticket-In connects clients with independent, freelance healthcare professionals for consultation. **Ticket-In is not an emergency service and does not replace in-person or emergency medical care.** If you are experiencing a life-threatening emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call emergency services immediately — do not wait for a Ticket-In consultation.

Ticket-In is a **platform**, not a healthcare provider itself. The healthcare professionals using this platform operate as independent freelance practitioners, not as employees or agents of Ticket-In. Clinical judgment, diagnosis, and treatment decisions are made independently by the professional handling your case — Ticket-In does not direct, supervise, or guarantee any specific clinical outcome.

## 2. Who can use Ticket-In

**Clients:** Ticket-In's check-in process does not currently verify age. **If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian assist with or be aware of your use of this service.** We are working to build proper age verification and parental consent into the platform; until that exists, please treat this as a real limitation, not a guarantee that the service is appropriate for use by minors without adult involvement.

**Healthcare professionals (staff):** To register as staff, you must provide accurate information about your professional license/registration number and issuing institution. **Providing false credential information is a serious violation of these Terms** and may result in immediate account suspension and, where appropriate, reporting to the relevant professional licensing body (such as the Cameroon Medical Council or the Ordre National des Infirmiers, Infirmières et Sages-Femmes du Cameroun).

Be aware: admin verification of staff credentials is a good-faith review of the information you provide. It does not currently include independent, real-time confirmation against the issuing institution's own records. We are working toward stronger verification over time.

## 3. How the service works

1. A client checks in, describing their situation, and receives a code to check status later
2. Payment (currently 1,600 XAF per consultation) is submitted manually via mobile money
3. Once payment is confirmed by platform admin, the consultation becomes available for a verified staff member to claim
4. The staff member who claims it provides the consultation and, upon completion, writes a plain-language summary for the client and separate clinical notes for the platform's records

## 4. Payments

- Consultation fee: 1,600 XAF, submitted via manual mobile money transfer, confirmed by platform admin
- Payments are not currently automated — there may be a delay between submitting payment and your consultation opening, while admin confirms it manually
- We do not currently offer refunds for consultations that have been claimed and completed. If a payment issue occurs before your consultation is claimed, contact us at the details below

- Clinical decisions made by staff on this platform are made independently and are not influenced by platform commercial interests

- Emergency situations are flagged internally when a client reports high severity, and are shown to platform admin for follow-up — **this internal flagging is a safety aid, not a guarantee of rapid response, and does not substitute for you seeking emergency care directly if your situation is serious**
