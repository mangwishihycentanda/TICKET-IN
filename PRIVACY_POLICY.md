This is a **good-faith interim Privacy Policy**, written to accurately describe what Ticket-In actually collects and does with your information today. It is **not** a substitute for formal authorization from Cameroon's Personal Data Protection Authority under Law No. 2024/017, which — because Ticket-In processes health data, a legally "sensitive" category — is required before this kind of processing can lawfully continue at any real scale. ## 1. Who we are

Ticket-In is a freelance healthcare consultation platform connecting clients with independent, freelance healthcare professionals in Cameroon.

## 2. What we collect

**If you check in as a client:**
- Your phone number
- The health information you provide in the check-in form (onset, location, duration, character, aggravating/relieving factors, timing, severity, and anything else you write)
- A system-generated code used to look up your ticket later (we do not require an account, name, or email)
- Payment reference information you submit when paying for a consultation (the mobile money transaction reference you provide — we do not process or store your mobile money account details themselves)

**If you register as a healthcare professional (staff):**
- Your name, email, and password
- Your professional license/registration number, issuing institution, and specialty
- Records of which tickets you claim and resolve, and the clinical notes and assessments you write for each case

**What we do NOT currently collect:**
- We do not ask your name, age, or any identity document as a client — check-in is deliberately account-free
- We do not currently verify your age. **If you are under 18, please involve a parent or guardian before using this service** — we do not yet have a way to obtain the parental consent that Cameroonian law requires for processing a minor's data, and this is a real, acknowledged gap we are working to close.

## 3. How we use your information

- Your check-in information is shown to the freelance professional who claims your ticket, so they can assess your situation
- A separate, plain-language summary is written by that professional specifically for you to read — your full clinical notes (assessment, diagnosis, plan) are kept internal to healthcare staff and platform admin, and are never shown to you directly in raw form
- Your phone number and code are used solely to let you look up your own ticket status later, and to let platform admin follow up with you directly if needed (for example, on an unpaid consultation flagged as urgent)
- Payment reference information is used only to confirm your payment and activate your consultation

## 4. Who can see your information

- **The staff member who claims your ticket** can see your check-in details and write clinical notes
- **Platform administrators** can see ticket details, payment information, and clinical notes, for the purpose of running the platform, confirming payments, and following up on cases that need attention
- **No other client, and no unverified staff member, can see your information.** Clinical notes are technically restricted (at the database level) to the staff member who wrote them and platform admins — this is enforced by the platform's access-control rules, not just a policy promise
- We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising

## 5. Where your information is stored

Ticket-In's data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database provider. At this time, this likely means your data is stored on servers outside Cameroon. Cross-border data transfer under Cameroonian law requires separate authorization from the Personal Data Protection Authority, which — like the general processing authorization above — has not yet been obtained. We are working to confirm and formalize this.

## 6. How long we keep your information

We have not yet set a formal data retention policy. Until we do, please assume your check-in and consultation records are retained indefinitely. This is an open item we intend to resolve as part of full legal compliance.

We do not process manual mobile money payments automatically. When you submit a payment, you send money directly via mobile money to an account we provide, and separately tell us the transaction reference. We do not have access to your mobile money account, PIN, or balance.
